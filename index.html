Packages

Six tiers. One we'll find for you.

From a clean two-speaker setup to full production with dancers and live musicians. We'll match the package to your venue, your guest count, and your budget.

Basic For intimate weddings and gatherings of 75 or fewer. Deluxe The Long Island standard. DJ on the music, MC working the room. Super Deluxe Same team from ceremony through last dance, with the illuminated booth. Premiere Towers, intelligent lights, dedicated tech. Supreme Adds live event photography on screens through the night. Grand Full production, plus a live sax or percussionist on the reception.
