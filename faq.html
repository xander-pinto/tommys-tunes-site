Questions

The things couples ask us most.

If what you want to know isn't here, call 631-732-5886 and ask. We would rather answer it than have you guess.

How much does a DJ cost?

We quote every night individually rather than publishing a price list, because a 75-guest afternoon and a 250-guest Saturday in a ballroom aren't the same job. Send us your date and venue and you'll have a real quote inside one business day.

Price comes up unprompted in more than twenty of our 250 reviews, and every one of those is five stars.

What happens after I check my date?

Someone from the office calls you back within one business day. A real person, not an automated reply with a price list attached.

From there you either come and sit down with us or do the whole thing over the phone, whichever suits you. We walk through the night start to finish and quote it from there. Checking a date doesn't commit you to anything.

How far in advance should we book?

As early as you can. There's no cutoff and we'll always try, but the further out you book, the more likely you are to get both your date and the specific DJ and MC you want.

Popular Saturdays go first, and so do the people couples ask for by name.

Can we meet our DJ before the wedding?

Yes. You can come to the Selden showroom to see the setups and hear the sound, meet us at one of our bridal showcases at Long Island venues, or book an appointment at Selden or at the Melville office.

Every person on our roster is named on this site with their own page, so you know who you're getting before you book.

Is the DJ also the MC?

It depends on the package. Basic is a one-person DJ/MC. From Deluxe upward you get two named people: a DJ focused entirely on the music, and a dedicated MC working the room and your timeline.

Splitting the roles lets each person do one job well, and it's the difference you feel on the dance floor.

What happens if our DJ gets sick?

You're covered either way. In practice it almost never comes up, because people here tend to work through it.

If someone genuinely can't make your night, we have more than fifty people on the roster and we put another one of our own on it. You won't be left without a DJ, and we won't be scrambling to find a stranger.

Are you insured?

Yes. If your venue asks for insurance documentation from your vendors, tell us and we'll take care of it.

Do you cover the ceremony and cocktail hour?

Yes. Super Deluxe and above include DJ music for the ceremony or cocktail hour as well as the reception. If either is in a separate space, you can add a second DJ for that room to any package.

Can we give you a playlist? Will you take requests?

Yes to both. Every package includes a custom playlist consultation, and there's a music selections form on the forms page for your must-plays, your do-not-plays, and the song for every formal moment.

On the night our DJs read the room and work your list into whatever is actually keeping people dancing. You can also browse our playlists by moment to start from.

Do you have Spanish-speaking MCs?

Yes. We have bilingual MCs who work Spanish-English weddings and Quinceañeras regularly, and bilingual talent in any language is something we actively hire for. Tell us what you need when you check your date.

Do you do more than DJ and MC?

Forty-seven things, all of which pair with any package: live saxophonists, percussionists, string quartets and other musicians; four kinds of photo booth; audio and video phone booths; intelligent lighting and illuminated towers; cold sparklers; dancing on a cloud; and live event photography on screens through the night.

See everything we do.

What parts of Long Island do you cover?

All of it. Nassau, Suffolk, the Hamptons and the North Fork, from Manhasset to Montauk.

We've been working the same Long Island rooms for forty years, so in most cases we already know your venue's load-in doors, its maître d', and where to set up to make your dance floor look full from photo one.

What kinds of events do you work?

Weddings, Sweet 16s and Quinceañeras, Bar and Bat Mitzvahs, corporate events, and everything else: communions, graduations, birthdays and anniversaries.
