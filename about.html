About

One family. 40+ years on Long Island.

Tommy started Tommy's Tunes in 1985. Four decades later, his three sons run it with him.

  1. 1985

    One DJ and a lot of Saturdays.

    Tommy Masino loaded a car with records and gear and started booking Long Island weddings one Saturday at a time. No showroom. No team. Just a guy who could read a room and keep a dance floor full.

    Word got around. One party became a referral, the referral became a calendar, and the calendar became a business.

  2. Early years

    Tuxedos, a hand-painted banner, and a pair of JBLs.

    The gear has changed. The part where two guys in bow ties are the reason the room is full hasn't.

  3. 2001

    Three kids on the back of the truck.

    Joe, TJ, and Dom grew up inside this business, riding along to load-ins and watching their dad work a room. They never really left.

  4. Today

    40+ years on, still on the road most weekends.

    The company runs out of its Selden showroom, the home it grew into years ago, where couples can see the setups, hear the sound, and meet the people who'll actually be at their event.

    The team has grown from one DJ to more than fifty. Tommy's still out there most weekends. He has worked weddings for couples whose parents he DJ'd for, MC'd four-generation Mitzvahs, and lost count of the Quinceañeras.

    DJ companies are easy to start and most don't last five years. Tommy's Tunes has lasted forty for one reason: every event still goes out with someone whose name you can pronounce and whose face you've seen.
